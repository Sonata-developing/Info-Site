Miriam Alice Krite

Miriam Alice Krite, better known to her friends as Mimi, is a 16-year-old actress in Grandious and one of the main characters in the Grand Circus (an upcoming animated series). She is the last `lost child` to be found by the Great Magician at the age of 10. Miriam does not like to talk about her past, especially about her mother. She is a very sociable and pleasant person with whom people can be happy.
