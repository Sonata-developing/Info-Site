Grand Circus

There are many interesting places in the wonderful city of Girnevirse: the Night Butterfly cafe, restaurants, houses, but if you cross the road from the Night Butterfly, then you will notice a huge tent. Don't rush to go there, you won't be allowed there if it's not a clear day and time of the show. At the show, you can see the main and only stars: twins Emily and Amilia, best friends until death Ack and Sam, the same best friends Mimi and Andrew... Andrew, don't look at Mimi like that, it's strange. And of course, the king of this chaos, the emperor of fun, and just a good man The Grand Magician! But wait... Something is strange here... Do you hear it? It's like the whispering of a whole choir... Strange, people in robes are gathering again in a long-closed workshop... Well, the job of figuring this out belongs to our heroes, so we're waiting for their adventures and solutions!
